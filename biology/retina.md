# Content
* The retina is the innermost, [[light-sensitive]] layer of [[tissue]] of the [[eye]].
* The optics of the eye create a [[focused]] two-dimensional image of the visual world on the retina, which then processes that image within the retina and sends the nerve impulses along the [[optic-nerve|optic nerve]] to the  [[visual-cortex|visual cortex]] to create [[visual-perception|visual perception]].
* The retina serves a function that is in many ways analogous to that of the [[film]] or [[image-sensor|image sensor]] in a [[camera]].
