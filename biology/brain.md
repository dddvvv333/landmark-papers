# Content
* The brain is an [[organ|Organ]] that serves as the center of the [[nervous-system|nervous system]] in all vertebrate and most invertebrate animasl.
* It consist of [[nervous-tissue|nervous tissue]].
* Is typically located in the [[head]], usually near organs for [[special-senses|special senses]] such as [[vision]], [[hearing]], and [[olfaction]].
* Is the most specialized organ, it is responsible for receiving [[information]] from the [[sensory-nervous-system|sensory nervous system]], processing that information and the coordination of [[motor-control|motor control]] ([[muscle]] activity) and the [[endocrine-system|endocrine system]].
