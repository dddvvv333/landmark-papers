# Content
* Several criteria to define this:
	1) Spatiotemporal existence: Abstract objects exist outside [[space-time]].
	2) Causal influence: Concrete objects can cause and be affected by other entities.
	3) Methaphysical relation: General concepts or categories.
	4) Ontological domain: Concrete belong to the physical realm (or both physical and mental realms), whereas abstract objects belong to neither.
